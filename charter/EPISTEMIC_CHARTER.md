## 1. Mission: Escaping the Primate Prior Without Breaking Empirical Reality

Human physics is a triumph of predictive engineering built atop **Pleistocene perceptual priors**. Biological brains evolved on an African savanna at low velocities ($v \ll c$), moderate gravitational potentials ($\Phi/c^2 \sim 10^{-9}$), high decoherence ($T \sim 300\text{ K}$), and macroscopic action scales ($S \gg \hbar$). To survive, a biological organism had to compress reality into a specific user-interface:
1. **3D Container Bias**: Treating 3D Euclidean space as the fundamental stage and "objects" (rocks, predators, particles) as localized marbles moving through it.
2. **Causal-Arrow Bias**: Because biological survival requires storing low-entropy memories of the past to select actions for the future, humans treat *forward-evolving time* $t$ as an external universal river, imposing initial conditions while ignoring future boundary conditions.
3. **Quantization Hammer**: Having succeeded at quantizing electromagnetic waves in a fixed background box into spin-1 photons, physicists spent 90 years trying to quantize the *box itself* into spin-2 gravitons—even though general covariance says background coordinates have no physical meaning.
4. **Substance Fallacy**: Whenever an equation deviates from expectations at new scales, humans invent an invisible substance ("ether", "vulcan", "cold dark matter WIMPs", "cosmological constant fluid") rather than questioning the constitutive assumptions of inertia, time, and horizon information.
